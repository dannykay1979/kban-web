KBAN Weekly Rota

Submit the rota for the week

One rota per depot per week, due by Friday for the following week. Once you submit it is live. You can come back and resubmit to correct it. Day to day changes go on the Daily Changes page, not here.
New starter: choose "New starter" in the driver box and type their name. Leaver: set Starter / Leaver to Leaver with the last day. Both are emailed to the office so the driver register is updated.
