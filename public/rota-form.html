Weekly Rota

KBAN Logistics

Rota details

One rota per depot per week, due by Friday for the following week. Check every driver, route, arrival time, fuel card and day before you submit. Once submitted it is live. You can reopen the same week and resubmit to correct it. Day to day changes go on the Daily Changes page, not here.
New starter: choose "New starter" in the driver box and type their name. Leaver: set Starter / Leaver to Leaver with the last day. Both are emailed to the office so the driver register is updated.
