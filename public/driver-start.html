KBAN Driver App

Start guide for drivers

1. Watch the video

Under 2 minutes. It shows a full start of day, vehicle check and end of day.

2. Open the app

Open the KBAN driver app

Log in with your name, depot and the personal PIN in your email. Do not share your PIN.

3. Save it to your phone

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the driver app page.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the driver app page.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

4. Each day

  1. Enter your route or ID: route ID (DPD), route number (FedEx), driver ID or temp ID (DHL).
  2. Complete your vehicle check with photos.
  3. Finish start of day before you leave the depot.
  4. Complete end of day when you return.

Stuck? Tell your lead or reply to the email you were sent and we will sort it the same day.
